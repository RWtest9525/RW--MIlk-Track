Account & Data Deletion Request

RW-Milk Tracker • Google Play Data Safety Compliant

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements, RW-Milk Tracker provides users with full control over their personal data, including permanent deletion of accounts and all associated data records.

⚠️ Permanent Action Warning

Deleting your account is permanent. All your delivery logs, vendor profiles, price settings, and payment ledger history will be permanently deleted from Cloud Firestore.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Instant)

Open RW-Milk Tracker App → Navigate to Settings (Profile Tab) → Scroll to Legal & Data Privacy → Tap Delete Account & Data → Confirm Deletion.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request (Support Ticket)

If you are unable to access the mobile app, send an email from your registered email address to support@rw-milk-tracker.com with the subject line "ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST". Account deletion will be processed within 24 hours.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

  • User Account Profile (Name, Email, Mobile Number, Profile Picture).
  • Dairy Vendor Settings & Custom Price / Litre configurations.
  • All Daily Delivery Logs and Monthly Summary Statements.
  • All Recorded Dues and Payment Ledger History.
