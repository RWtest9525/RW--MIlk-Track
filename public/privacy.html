Privacy Policy

RW-Milk Tracker • Google Play Policy Compliant
Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to RW-Milk Tracker. Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information when you use our mobile or web application.

1. Information We Collect

RW-Milk Tracker collects only necessary data required to provide daily milk delivery tracking and monthly invoice generation:

  • Account Credentials: Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number via Firebase Authentication.
  • Delivery Preferences: Dairy Vendor Name, Vendor Phone Number, Price Per Litre (₹), and Default Daily Milk Quantity.
  • Daily Logs: Daily milk delivery status (Delivered, Missed, Custom Quantity) and optional delivery notes.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for:

  • Synchronizing your daily milk consumption records securely across devices using Cloud Firestore.
  • Calculating monthly dues, total litres delivered, and generating WhatsApp text invoices or PDF statements.
  • Providing user profile and vendor settings management.

3. Data Security & Storage

All data is encrypted in transit and stored in Google Cloud Firestore infrastructure with strict security rules. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or milk logs to third parties.

4. Account and Data Deletion

Users have the right to request permanent deletion of their account and all associated delivery records at any time. You can initiate account deletion inside the app under Profile Settings or by visiting our Account Deletion Request Page.
